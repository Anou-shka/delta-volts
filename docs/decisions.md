# Analyst decision log

I make the investment calls; the code implements them. This log records each decision that shapes the model, when it was made, and why. Assumptions that move the valuation by more than ~5% are decided here before they're built.

| # | Date | Decision | Why | Effect on the model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 16 Oct 2026 | **Anchor the AI data-centre revenue line only on Delta's own disclosures.** | The widely quoted split (AI power supplies >20% and AI cooling ~9% of FY2025 revenue) comes from press coverage of the 4Q25 call. It isn't in Delta's slides, and Delta published no 4Q25 transcript. A thesis built on an unverifiable figure won't hold up under questioning. | AI-line anchors (all from the 2Q26 meeting transcript):<br>• AI-related products **>25% of FY2026 revenue** (p.12);<br>• liquid cooling **~10% of FY2025 revenue** and **>12% of FY2026** (p.12);<br>• data-centre business **>50% of 1H26 revenue** (p.2).<br>The press figures stay in `sources.csv` as `TODO-SOURCE` and aren't used. |
| D2 | 16 Oct 2026 | **Wait for Q3 2026 results before locking the base period.** | Q3 results are expected around 29–30 Oct 2026. They will be the latest reviewed statements, and they add a quarter of AI-driven growth to the base. Locking now would mean revising every forecast two weeks later. | The base period moves from 1H26 to 9M26 when the Q3 2026 report is published. Until then, Jul–Sep monthly revenue (NT$197.1bn, unaudited) is the Q3 cross-check. The historical layer (M1) is final for FY2021–FY2025 and 1H26. |
| D3 | 16 Oct 2026 | Operating EBITDA (EBIT + D&A) for valuation, not Delta's presented EBITDA. | Delta's figure (PBT + finance costs + D&A) includes non-operating gains, which don't belong in an EV multiple. | FY2025 EBITDA NT$111.8bn (model) vs NT$117.9bn (Delta's slides). Both are shown. |
| D4 | 16 Oct 2026 | Capex = cash paid for PPE + intangibles (cash flow statement). | It's audited and consistent across years. Management (NT$46.6bn) and press (NT$46.1bn) figures use undisclosed definitions. | FY2025 capex NT$47.2bn. |
| D5 | 16 Oct 2026 | Segment history on the latest restated basis from FY2023; FY2021–FY2022 on the old 3-segment basis. | Delta carved Mobility out of Power Electronics in the FY2024 report and reclassified products again in FY2025. Restating earlier years myself would mean inventing an allocation. | Segment growth rates before FY2023 are shown for Power Electronics including Mobility. |

| D6 | 16 Nov 2026 | **Use the 3Q26 earnings slides and transcript for Q3; roll the balance sheet forward from FY2025A.** | Delta held its 3Q26 results meeting on 29 Oct 2026, but the English Q3 financial statements were not yet on its IR site on 16 Nov (they usually lag the Chinese filing). The slides are a primary company source and tie to the audited 1H26 note to within NT$1m for every segment. | 3Q26A and 9M26A segment revenue and profit (and group PBT, tax, NCI, net income, EPS) come from the slides (`data/slides_3q26.csv`). FY2026E = 9M26 actual + 4Q26E. The forecast balance sheet starts from audited FY2025A; the 30 Sep 2026 balance sheet will be added when the English report is published. |
| D7 | 16 Nov 2026 | **Update the AI anchors to management's 3Q26 clarification.** | On the 3Q26 call the CFO said the earlier "25%" refers to AI *power* solutions only, and that AI data-centre businesses in total are about 50% of revenue. Liquid cooling was NT$25bn in 3Q26 (~13% of revenue), with a similar share for FY2026. | FY2026E AI data-centre revenue = 50% of revenue: power 25%, liquid cooling 13%, other 12% (the balance). Supersedes the D1 anchors from the 2Q26 call. |
| D8 | 16 Nov 2026 | **Drive the AI line with a two-link chain: AI data-centre capex growth × Delta's content/share gain.** | Public data can't support a full rack-shipments × content-per-rack chain for Delta: Delta discloses neither units nor content per rack. Management does quote hyperscaler capex (US$700–800bn in 2026, >US$1tn in 2027). | For each AI line, growth = (1 + capex growth) × (1 + content gain) − 1. Allocation of the AI lines between Power Electronics and Infrastructure isn't disclosed and is an analyst input. |
| D9 | 16 Nov 2026 | **Segment margins drive EBIT; SG&A is the balancing item.** | Delta discloses segment operating profit, so margins by segment capture the mix effect of AI growth directly. Gross margin (management: "~35% reasonable") and R&D % are separate inputs; SG&A balances to the segment-built EBIT. | A forecast sanity check flags SG&A outside 5–15% of revenue. Depreciation affects cash flow and PP&E, not EBIT, because segment margins are after D&A. |
| D10 | 16 Nov 2026 | **One assumptions file, three scenarios.** | The analyst sets every driver in one place; the workbook and (in M4) the Python mirror build from the same file, so they can't drift apart. | `model/assumptions.csv`: 40 drivers × Bear / Base / Bull × FY2026E–FY2030E, each with a one-line rationale. Drafts are marked `[ANALYST TO REVIEW]`. |
